How to Play

Find groups of four words that share something in common.

Select four words and tap Submit to check if they form a group. You get 4 mistakes before the game ends.

Each puzzle has exactly four groups. The color indicates difficulty:

Easiest → Hardest

You can also create your own puzzle — pick four groups of four words, and share the link with friends!
